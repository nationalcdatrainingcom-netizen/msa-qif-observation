The Program

Everything a mentoring program needs, in one place

Most programs know mentoring works. What's hard is keeping it going week after week. MSA gives mentors, mentees and directors a shared plan and a simple way to stay on track.

✦

Mentor training

Twelve weekly modules with real classroom scenarios teach mentors how to coach teachers with different personalities and working styles.

✎

Weekly reflections

Mentors and mentees each reflect on the week's focus, so every meeting starts from what's actually happening in the classroom.

◎

Classroom observations

The Quality Interaction Framework gives mentors a clear, consistent way to observe teacher–child interactions and talk about them.

▤

Director dashboard

Directors see each pair's progress at a glance and can step in early when a pair falls behind.

★

Mentor certification

Mentors who finish the training can earn the annual MSA Certified Mentor credential, with a printable certificate.

▶

Coaching call library

Recorded coaching calls and resources stay available, so new mentors can catch up and experienced ones can revisit.

Free Research Trial

Join the upcoming 3-month research trial

This trial is part of Mary Wardlaw's doctoral research on the impact of mentoring in early childhood programs. Participating programs use MSA free for 12 weeks. In return, the data gathered, including surveys before and after the program, is used in the research.

Who can take part

  • A licensed early childhood program in the United States.
  • A privately owned program.
  • At least 2 classrooms serving children 2½ years and older.
  • One classroom uses MSA and one does not, so the research can compare the two.
  • Staff in both classrooms complete the surveys before and after the program.
  • The director agrees to provide time every week for the full 12 weeks for the mentor and mentee to meet.
Taking part is voluntary. Results are reported in summary form, without naming individual programs or staff. Accepted programs will receive full research details and consent forms before the trial begins.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much time does the program take each week?

Each mentoring pair meets once a week, and mentors and mentees each complete a short reflection. Directors set aside that weekly meeting time for the 12 weeks.

Why does one classroom not use MSA in the research trial?

Comparing a classroom that uses MSA with one that doesn't lets the research measure what difference mentoring actually makes. Both classrooms complete the same surveys.

What happens after the 3-month trial?

When the trial ends, programs can continue with a paid subscription. There's no obligation to continue.

Can we subscribe without joining the research?

Yes. A subscription gives your program full access with no research requirements, and you can start at any time.

Our program doesn't meet the trial criteria. Can we still use MSA?

Yes. The criteria only apply to the free research trial. Any program can subscribe.
