Mentor training for preschool teachers

Train one great mentor. Grow teacher after teacher.

Mentor Success Academy (MSA) is a 12-week mentor training program that teaches experienced preschool teachers how to mentor and coach other early childhood teachers, built on MSA’s LEAP framework. Your strong teachers become skilled, confident mentors: each one learns how to observe, give feedback and coach, and practices those skills right away with a colleague in your program. The skills stay with the mentor, ready for the next teacher, and the next.

Who's involved

Mentors are trained. Your whole program benefits.

Mentors

The ones being trained. Experienced teachers learn how to observe, give feedback and coach, then earn MSA certification and keep those skills for every teacher they support next.

Mentees

The teachers your mentors support. They meet with their mentor each week and get steady, personal support while the mentor puts new skills into practice.

Directors

Choose the teachers to train as mentors, protect weekly meeting time and build a team that can grow its own great teachers.
