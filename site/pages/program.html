The 12-week Mentor Success Academy program, week by week

The Mentor Success Academy program runs for 12 weeks in three four-week focus areas: Emotional Climate & Responsiveness (weeks 1–4), Classroom Organization (weeks 5–8) and Instructional Support (weeks 9–12). Each week has one online training module with 16 coaching scenarios, one for each profile in MSA’s LEAP framework, for 192 scenarios in all.

What’s included

Everything a mentor needs to learn, practice and grow

Most programs know mentoring works. What's hard is knowing how to mentor well and keeping it going week after week. MSA gives mentors the training, and gives mentors, mentees and directors a shared plan to stay on track.

✦

Mentor training

The heart of MSA. Twelve weekly modules with real classroom scenarios teach mentors how to coach teachers with different personalities and working styles, using MSA’s LEAP framework (Leadership Expression Assessment Profile).

✎

Weekly reflections

Mentors and their mentees each reflect on the week's focus, so every meeting starts from what's actually happening in the classroom.

◎

Classroom observations

The Quality Interaction Framework gives mentors a clear, consistent way to observe teacher–child interactions and talk about them.

▤

Director dashboard

Directors see each mentor's training and mentoring progress at a glance and can step in early when someone falls behind.

★

Mentor certification

After all 12 modules, mentors can take a 12-scenario certification test, one scenario from each week. Passing earns the annual MSA Certified Mentor credential, with a printable certificate.

▶

Coaching call library

Recorded coaching calls and resources stay available, so new mentors can catch up and experienced ones can revisit.
